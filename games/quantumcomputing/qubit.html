How to Play Qubit

A cooperative quantum deduction game

The Goal

Your team shares one secret Quantum Code — a single cell hidden somewhere on the 16×16 Quantum Matrix. Work together to deduce it and call a correct Measurement before the system decoheres.

Your Clues

  • Each player is dealt 1–2 private Wavelength Clues — only true for the hidden code, and only visible to you.
  • Everyone shares one public Entanglement Clue, comparing two qubit positions (same or different).

Communication Rules

Players may talk freely about strategy and about anything visibly on the board — including reasoning out loud about other players' tokens. Three things are never allowed:

  • Never state, describe, or hint at the content of your own Wavelength Clue.
  • Never confirm or deny anything about your clue except through the formal Verify step of an active player's turn — no answering side questions about it outside of being properly asked.
  • Never reference or explain the tokens you personally placed on the board — though everyone else is free to observe, discuss, and reason about any tokens on the board, including yours.

On Your Turn

  1. Draw an Environment card. Revealed automatically at the start of your turn, before you propose:
    • Coherence (Maintained). Play continues as normal — you'll choose a teammate to Verify after proposing.
    • Constructive Cascade. Every other player will verify the cell at once after you propose — no need to choose.
    • Noise. A decoherence strike. Otherwise your turn plays out as normal — you'll still choose a teammate to Verify. Only the 3rd Noise card ends the mission immediately, before Verify.
  2. Propose. Pick any cell that matches ALL of your own clues. Your token lands there — always your player color in a circle with a checkmark, since it's guaranteed to match.
  3. Verify by another player. Choose one teammate to Verify — they place a token on that same cell: a circle with a checkmark if it matches their clue, a square with an X if it doesn't. (Automatic for everyone on Cascade; skipped only if the 3rd Noise card just ended the game.)
  4. Play passes to the next player, who immediately draws the next Environment card.

Deduction

Use the growing pattern of circle checkmarks (match) and square X's (no match) tokens across the board, plus your own “Highlight valid cells” toggle, to narrow down which cell must be the true code.

Calling a Measurement

On your turn, instead of proposing, any human player may call a Measurement on the cell they believe is the answer.

Measurement is final. Guess right and the team wins instantly. Guess wrong and the team loses instantly.

Win or Lose

  • Win. Correctly call Measurement on the hidden Quantum Code.
  • Lose. 3 Noise cards are drawn (full decoherence), or a Measurement is called on the wrong cell.
