The AI Literacy Problem

E-learning completed on Friday is forgotten by Monday.

Most AI literacy training doesn't work. It's slides people sit through, or modules people click through, and then it's over. The vocabulary doesn't stick because it was never connected to a real decision. The concepts don't land because nobody ever felt the consequences of getting them wrong.

What builds real AI fluency is experience: making decisions, seeing what happens, and doing it again. Building a neural network from scratch, choosing what to train on, watching a model fail when you overfit it. That's when the concepts become intuition. And the debrief that follows is when the facilitator turns that experience into understanding your team can actually use.

When that session is run by professionals who know the material deeply and know how to hold a room, the quality of the learning is different. We've run these sessions in large venues, with mixed audiences, and at high-stakes moments. That experience is what we bring in.
